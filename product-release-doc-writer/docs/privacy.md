# Product Release Doc Writer — privacy information

Effective: 6 October 2026. Publisher: Manish Jha.

## What the package contains

Product Release Doc Writer is an instruction-only skill. It bundles writing instructions, reference guides and Markdown templates. It does not include an MCP server, connected service, telemetry, analytics, account system or publisher-operated storage.

## Inputs and purpose

You supply the product facts, source documents, templates and examples needed to draft or revise a release document. Those inputs may contain personal information such as names of release owners. The skill uses relevant supplied information to prepare the requested document and distinguish facts, uncertainties and proposed actions. Supply only information you are permitted to use in your chosen application.

## Processing, recipients and retention

The application running the skill processes your inputs and generated documents under its own privacy terms, data controls and retention settings. The publisher does not receive your conversation or documents through this package and does not maintain a separate copy or retention period for them. The package has no built-in mechanism to send these inputs to the publisher or another external service.

If you separately authorize tools or connections in your application, their own permissions and data practices apply. These instructions do not establish those permissions. No claim is made here about an application's model training, storage location or retention period.

## Your controls

You choose the inputs, the audience and which facts are public. You can remove information before supplying it, disable or uninstall the skill, and use the host application's controls to manage or delete conversations and files. Disabling or uninstalling the skill does not itself delete data retained by the host application.

## Support

Use the [support page](support.md) for questions about this package. GitHub issues are public and may contain your username and any text or files you post; do not post confidential product documents, personal information, credentials or private conversations there. Support submissions are handled through GitHub and remain subject to GitHub's own controls and retention practices. The publisher may read them to respond to the issue.
