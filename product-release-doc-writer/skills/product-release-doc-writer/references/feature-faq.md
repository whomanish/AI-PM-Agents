# Feature FAQ

Use this guide primarily for internal Sales, Customer Success, and other customer-facing teams preparing customer conversations. Distinguish customer-ready answers from internal-only notes so teams can reuse approved language safely. Address questions that help a reader understand what the capability does, who can access it, how to use it, its supported limits, and how it relates to relevant workflows.

Answer from supplied evidence. State availability, packaging, pricing, comparative claims, proof, customer feedback, and roadmap only when supported and suitable for the audience. Do not infer an answer from a heading or from common product patterns. For an unknown answer, say what is not confirmed, omit an optional question, or leave a specific placeholder; do not convert uncertainty into a promise or a blanket negative. Label inferred follow-ups as proposals or open questions, never as confirmed team assignments.

Keep answers concise and specific. Separate internal-only notes from customer-facing copy, and apply the audience-boundary guidance before including mixed-audience information.
