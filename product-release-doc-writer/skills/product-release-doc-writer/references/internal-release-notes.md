# Internal Release Notes

Use this guide for cross-functional internal alignment. Select only sections that help readers understand the release and act on it. Depending on available evidence, useful topics include:

- What changed and who or what is affected.
- Availability and rollout context.
- Readiness, dependencies, and known risks.
- Decisions, enablement needs, measures, and next actions.

State confirmed facts directly. Distinguish current state from proposals, open questions, and unresolved conflicts. Only actions and follow-ups explicitly supplied as decisions or commitments are confirmed actions; label inferred follow-ups as proposals or open questions. Give an owner or date only when supplied, and treat a missing owner as unknown rather than unassigned. Do not infer an internal process, approval role, rollout phase, metric, or decision from convention. Keep this artifact internal; do not reuse its internal details in public copy without confirmation that they are public.

For a revision, retain verified material and requested structure while updating facts from the user's latest explicit corrections. Use the selected template's headings and format.
